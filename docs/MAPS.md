360x520 px phone play area. **With slop** adds the 14 px on each side that the game forgives
around the right region, and the verdict is based on it: `ok` is 44 px or more, `tight` is 24 to
43 px, `needs-zoom` is under 24 px. The last column lists regions that are under 24 px before
